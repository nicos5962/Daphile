PLUGIN_DAPHILE
	EN	Daphile

PLUGIN_DAPHILE_DESC
	EN	The plugin for Daphile extras.

DSD
	EN	DSD

PLUGIN_DAPHILE_USEDOP
	EN	Enable DSD-over-PCM

PLUGIN_DAPHILE_USEDOP_DESC
	EN	If the DAC of this player supports DSD-over-PCM standard you should enable this in order to get native DSD playback instead of conversion to PCM. You must also ensure that Volume Control setting for this player is "Output level is fixed at 100%".

PLUGIN_DAPHILE_PCMDSF
	EN	Enable PCM to DSD conversion

PLUGIN_DAPHILE_PCMDSF_DESC
	EN	All possible PCM streams will be converted to DSD (dsf).

HLS
	EN	HTTP Live Streaming (HLS)

PLUGIN_DAPHILE_OVERRIDE
	EN	Use Daphile transcoding rules

PLUGIN_DAPHILE_OVERRIDE_DESC
	EN	Replace the stock convert.conf rules with the Daphile Transcode/Decode helpers (requires sox, ffmpeg, ffprobe and jq). Applied immediately; disabling restores the stock rules.
